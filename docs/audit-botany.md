# Botany and plant-cell simulation audit (NCERT-aligned)

Scope: Biology sims in `bio1.js` to `bio5.js` that are botany, plant physiology, cell biology, cell cycle or biomolecules. Zoology, genetics, evolution, biotech and general population ecology belong to the other audit.
Method: code reading only. Nothing was rendered or run, so visual findings are inferred from coordinates and sizes in the code. Facts are checked against my knowledge of NCERT Class 11/12 Biology. I had no copy of the textbook, so every "matches NCERT" statement is unverified against the book itself.
Note: `docs/simulation-quality-audit.md` (the inventory named in the task) does not exist in this worktree. The scope list below was built from the `add({...})` calls.

Counts: 34 audited, 24 with at least one confirmed defect, 10 with none confirmed, 6 with a "flag for review" item (4 of those also have confirmed defects).
Severity: high = wrong science that teaches a wrong mechanism, direction or sequence. medium = misleading, contradictory or broken state. low = cosmetic or minor wording. none = nothing confirmed. flag = unverified item needing a check against the book.
Line numbers refer to the files at this commit.
