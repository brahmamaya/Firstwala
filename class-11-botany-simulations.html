Class 11 Botany simulations

32 free interactive Class 11 Botany simulations covering 10 NCERT chapters, including Plant Kingdom, Anatomy of Flowering Plants, Biological Classification. Change the variables, watch the result, and learn for school exams, JEE Main and NEET.

Plant Kingdom (3 simulations)

  • Green, brown and red algae — Compare the three classes of algae by pigments, stored food, cell wall and flagella (NCERT Table 3.1).
  • Alternation of generations — Follow plant life cycles round the loop of haploid (n) and diploid (2n) phases. Compare which phase dominates.
  • From algae to flowering plants — Step through the five plant groups and see how vascular tissue, seeds and the dominant generation change.

Anatomy of Flowering Plants (3 simulations)

  • Secondary growth and annual rings — Let a dicot tree grow year by year. Cambium adds wide spring wood and narrow, dense autumn wood each year.
  • Dicot vs monocot stem — Compare transverse sections of dicot and monocot stems: arrangement and type of vascular bundles.
  • Stomata: guard cells at work — Change the turgor of the guard cells and watch the stomatal pore open and close.

Cell Cycle and Cell Division (3 simulations)
