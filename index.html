How to use Physica

Explore

  • Pick a chapter on the left, then a simulation tab. Tabs marked 3D are full 3D scenes.
  • Drag a 3D stage to orbit the camera. Pinch, Ctrl + scroll or the − / + buttons zoom. Double-click to reset.
  • Use − / + beside each slider for fine steps; hold to repeat. Type exact values in the boxes.
  • Share setup copies a link that reopens this experiment with your exact values.
