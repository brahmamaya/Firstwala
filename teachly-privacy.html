Privacy policy

Teachly by Physica · Last updated 20 November 2026

In short
  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • Your lessons, notebooks and recordings stay on your own device. We never receive them.
  • The Google Play version of the Android app has no internet permission at all. The website version uses the internet only to load Teachly's own files.

1. What stays on your device

Everything you make in Teachly is saved only on the device you are using, in the storage of your browser or of the Teachly Android app:

  • notebooks, pages, writing, shapes, graphs, text, folders and the Trash;
  • PDF files, PowerPoint files and pictures you open in Teachly (they are read on your device and are not uploaded);
  • each picture on a page is kept on your device as a separate file, and when Teachly updates an older notebook to the newest format it keeps an untouched copy of the old version on your device; both are removed when the notebook is deleted for good;
  • a copy of each PDF file you open, and pictures of its pages made when you look at them, kept with the notebook so that big books open fast and Save as PDF can reuse the real pages (small and sharp); they are removed when the notebooks using them are deleted for good;
  • your settings (for example pen colours, recent colours, board colour, graph settings, recording quality) and the place where you stopped, so Teachly can open where you left off;
  • names you type into the name picker, and team names and scores in the scoreboard.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else.

2. What we do not collect

Teachly does not ask for or collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, location, photos from your gallery (only a file you choose yourself is opened), or any information about your students. Teachly has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking cookies, and we do not sell or share any data, because we do not have any.

3. When Teachly uses the internet

  • No other websites. Teachly's fonts, pictures and code are part of Teachly itself. The app never asks Google, a content network or any other site for anything while you use it, and it tells the browser not to pass on where you came from. So no other website learns your device's address or anything about your use.
  • Website version: your browser downloads Teachly's files from the website host. Like every website host, it receives the technical details needed to send the files (such as your IP address, browser type and time) and may keep them in its own logs under its own privacy policy. Teachly adds nothing to this. After the first visit Teachly also works without internet.
  • Android app from Google Play: the app does not use the internet. It contains all its files, and Google Play delivers updates. (Only a copy of the app installed outside Google Play may check the website for newer app files; nothing about you or your lessons is sent.)
  • Share Teachly: only when you tap it, your device's share menu opens with a link to physica.in.
  • Links: a web link you open from Teachly opens in your browser.

4. Microphone

The microphone is used only for Record lesson, only after you start a recording and allow it. The sound goes into the video that is saved on your device. It is not sent anywhere.

Teachly does not use the camera at all. The Android app from Google Play asks for one permission only: microphone, and only when you tap the lesson-recording feature. You can refuse the microphone, or take it back at any time in your device settings; the rest of Teachly keeps working.

5. Files you save

PDFs, videos and pictures you save from Teachly are saved on your device (on Android in Downloads / Teachly; on iPad through the share menu, for example Save to Files). What happens to them after that is up to you.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Deleted notebooks go to the Trash; Empty trash removes them for good.
  • Uninstalling the Android app, or clearing this website's data in your browser, deletes everything Teachly saved on that device. The Android app keeps its data out of Android's cloud backup, so there is no copy elsewhere.
  • Safari on iPad and iPhone may remove the data of websites that have not been used for some time. Adding Teachly to the Home Screen avoids this. Save important lessons as PDF as well.
  • Because we never receive your data, we cannot recover it for you.

7. Security

Your lessons are protected by your device's own security, such as its screen lock. Anyone who can use your device can open Teachly and see your notebooks.

8. Children

Teachly is made for teachers. It collects no personal data from anyone, including students. Student names typed into the name picker or scoreboard stay only on that device.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be shown here with a new date.
