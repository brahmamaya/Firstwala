Equilibrium (4 simulations)

  • Buffer solutions (Henderson equation) — Mix acetic acid with sodium acetate and add a little strong acid or base; see how little the pH changes.
  • Le Chatelier’s principle (Haber process) — Disturb N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃ by changing pressure, temperature or adding reactant and watch the equilibrium shift.
  • pH of acids and bases — Choose an acid or base and its concentration; see the pH, the indicator colour and the ions in solution.
  • Solubility product and common ion effect — Dissolve AgCl in water or in NaCl solution and see how the common Cl⁻ ion lowers its solubility.
