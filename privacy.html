Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10-28 · Last updated: 28 October 2026

In short. Physica has no accounts and no server of its own. The simulation lab and the Test Series keep your settings, answers and scores only in your own browser. We do not collect your name, email or test results, we never sell data, we show no ads, and we do not track or profile students. Visit statistics (Google Analytics) are off unless you are 18 or older and turn them on.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

“Physica”, “we”, “us” is the individual who runs Physica as a sole proprietorship (no company is registered yet), address postal address available on request at easy@physica.in, operating physica.in. For personal data we collect through Physica we are the “Data Fiduciary” under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”) and we also follow the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under it. Contact: easy@physica.in.

This policy covers the whole of Physica: (a) the simulation lab and (b) the Test Series. Neither needs an account. We will update this policy before we change what we collect.

2. The simulation lab (no account)

Saved only on your device

These stay in your own browser (“local storage”) and are never sent to us: the name or nickname you give the AI Tutor, language and level, tutor stars, your mode, theme, favourite simulations and your visit-statistics choice. Clear your browser’s site data to remove them.

What you choose to send us

  • Feedback form: the name, country and message you type.
  • Help & queries form: the name, optional email, topic and message you type.

These are delivered through Google Forms and used only to reply to you and to improve Physica. Please do not include sensitive personal details.

Questions the tutor could not answer

If the AI Tutor cannot answer a question, the question text is sent anonymously (without your name) with the chapter, language and level, so we can write a good answer. It is limited to a few questions per day.

Voice

Speech comes from your device’s or browser’s own voices. Some browsers may process the spoken text on their own servers under their own privacy policies. The 🎤 button uses your browser’s speech recognition only when you press it. We do not receive or store any audio.

3. Test Series: what happens to your answers

Test Series runs entirely in your browser. The test papers are plain files on our website. When you answer, your answers, flags, time spent per question, start time and score are stored only in your browser’s local storage on your own device. They are never sent to us, and we cannot see them. There is no login, no name, no email address and no rank list.

  • Clearing your browser’s site data, using another browser or device, or private mode removes or hides your results.
  • The website’s hosting provider (section 4) may keep ordinary server logs (such as IP address and browser type) when your browser downloads the site and test files.
  • We do not collect phone numbers, addresses, government IDs, photos, location, contacts or microphone/camera access in Test Series. No payments are accepted.

4. Who handles your data for us

We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising. Providers involved:

  • GitHub Pages, which serves the website files and keeps its own server logs.
  • Google: Google Forms for the feedback and help forms; Google Analytics only if you opt in (section 6).

We may disclose data if required by law, a court or a government authority, or to protect people’s safety or our rights. Some providers (for example Google and GitHub) may process data outside India; the DPDP Act permits this except to countries the Government restricts.

5. Children (under 18)

Many Physica users are students under 18. For them we apply the DPDP Act’s child protections:

  • Because we collect no personal data in the lab or the Test Series, there is nothing to consent to or to store about a child. If we ever add accounts, we will first add verifiable parental consent and update this notice (the DPDP child-consent rules apply from 13 May 2027).
  • We do not track or monitor children’s behaviour, build profiles of them, or show them targeted advertising. There are no ads on Physica.
  • Visit statistics (Google Analytics): by default Google Analytics counts visits and returning visitors using its normal first-party cookie (it expires after 90 days). Google signals and ad personalisation are off. Nothing is counted while the Test Series is open, or if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. You can switch to “No cookies” (page views only) or turn everything off.

6. Cookies, local storage and visit statistics

Physica sets no cookies of its own. It uses your browser’s local storage for the settings in section 2 and for your Test Series answers and scores (section 3). Physica itself keeps no visit counters.

Visit statistics with Google Analytics. By default we use Google Analytics to see how many people visit Physica, how many are new and how many come back each day. It sets a first-party cookie that expires after 90 days. We switch off Google signals and ad personalisation, we send only the name of the page or simulation you open, and nothing you type, no answers and no scores. Nothing is counted while the Test Series is open, or if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. Google also receives the normal web request, which includes your IP address and browser details. We ask Google Analytics to mask IP addresses, and Google states that Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. You can choose “No cookies” (page views only, a new random number on every page load) or “Turn off”. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or teacher before using Physica with statistics on, or turn them off here. Change your choice at any time:

Non-registered users and students. Physica has no accounts, so nobody registers, and we do not collect personally identifiable information such as a name, email address, phone number, school or test result from anyone, including students. When visit statistics are on, Google Analytics receives only this: the name of the page or simulation opened, the time, the page address and the website you came from, your device type, browser and operating system, your approximate location (such as country or state) and, in the default mode, a random number kept in a first-party cookie to tell new visitors from returning ones. We use it only to see how many people visit and which simulations are used, to keep Physica working well. Read more about Google’s practices at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and see its opt-out add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

7. How we protect data

No system can be made perfectly secure, but Physica is built to hold as little as possible:

  • All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • No accounts, no passwords and no database, so there is no central store of student data to breach.
  • Strict browser security rules (Content Security Policy); no third-party scripts inside Test Series; the Test Series page is built without injecting HTML.
  • A watchdog checks that the live website is exactly what we published and that internal files are not exposed; secrets and private files are blocked from publishing automatically.

If something goes wrong. If a personal-data breach ever affects you we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires, and report cyber incidents to CERT-In within the time the law sets (currently 6 hours). To report a security problem, write to easy@physica.in (see security.txt). Please give us reasonable time to fix it before making it public.

8. How long we keep data

We keep no Test Series data. What is in your browser stays until you clear it. Form messages (feedback and help) are kept only as long as needed to reply and improve Physica, and are held in Google Forms under Google’s terms.

9. Your rights

You (or, for a child, the parent or guardian) may:

  • See and delete your Test Series answers and scores at any time: they are in your browser; clear this site’s data (browser settings → site data) to erase them.
  • Ask us about, correct or erase anything you sent through the feedback or help forms, or withdraw consent for analytics (switch above).
  • Grievance redressal (section 10), and nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to.

We acknowledge every complaint within 48 hours and aim to resolve it within 30 days. If you are not satisfied you may approach the Data Protection Board of India as the law permits.
