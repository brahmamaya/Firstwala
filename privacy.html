Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Physica is used by students, many of whom are under 18. We collect as little as possible. This policy explains what information is involved and your choices, in line with Indian law including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. What stays only on your device

These are saved in your own browser (“local storage”) and are never sent to us: the name or nickname you give the AI Tutor, your chosen language and level, tutor stars, your mode (Normal / Student / Teacher), theme and favourite simulations. You can delete them anytime by clearing your browser’s site data.

2. What you choose to send us

  • Feedback form: the name, country and message you type.
  • Help & queries form: the name, optional email, topic and message you type.

These are delivered to the Physica team through Google Forms and used only to reply to you and to improve Physica. Please don’t include sensitive personal details.

3. Questions the tutor could not answer

If the AI Tutor cannot answer a question, the question text is sent anonymously (without your name) together with the chapter, language and level, so that we can write a good answer. It is limited to a few questions per day.

4. Visit statistics

The main lab page uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which simulations are used, so we can improve them. Google may use cookies or similar technology for this. You can block it with your browser’s privacy settings or an extension; Physica still works.

5. The tutor’s voice and microphone

Speech is produced by your device’s or browser’s own voices. Some browsers (for example Edge “Natural” voices or Chrome’s Google voices) may process the spoken text on their own servers under their privacy policies. The 🎤 button uses your browser’s speech recognition only when you press it.

6. What we don’t do

  • No accounts, passwords or payments are collected on Physica.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not send your tutor conversations to any AI company.

7. Children

Physica is meant for learning. Students under 18 should use the forms with the knowledge of a parent, guardian or teacher. A parent or guardian may ask us to delete anything their child sent through our forms.

8. How long we keep data

Form messages are kept only as long as needed to reply and improve Physica, and are deleted on request.

9. Your rights and grievances

You may ask to see, correct or delete information you sent us, or raise a complaint. Use the Help & queries form (topic “Privacy or data request”) or email easy@physica.in. We aim to respond within 30 days.

10. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
