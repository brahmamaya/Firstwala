Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10-21 · Last updated: 21 October 2026

In short. The simulation lab works without an account and keeps its settings only in your browser. If you create a Test Series account we collect your email, name and test results to run the tests, and nothing more. We never sell data, we show no ads, and we do not track or profile students. Students under 18 need a parent or guardian’s agreement. You can download or delete your data yourself at any time.

2. The simulation lab (no account)

Saved only on your device

These stay in your own browser (“local storage”) and are never sent to us: the name or nickname you give the AI Tutor, language and level, tutor stars, your mode, theme, favourite simulations and your visit-statistics choice. Clear your browser’s site data to remove them.

What you choose to send us

  • Feedback form: the name, country and message you type.
  • Help & queries form: the name, optional email, topic and message you type.

These are delivered through Google Forms and used only to reply to you and to improve Physica. Please do not include sensitive personal details.

Questions the tutor could not answer

If the AI Tutor cannot answer a question, the question text is sent anonymously (without your name) with the chapter, language and level, so we can write a good answer. It is limited to a few questions per day.

Voice

Speech comes from your device’s or browser’s own voices. Some browsers may process the spoken text on their own servers under their own privacy policies. The 🎤 button uses your browser’s speech recognition only when you press it. We do not receive or store any audio.

5. Children (under 18)

Many Physica users are students under 18. For them we apply the DPDP Act’s child protections:

  • A parent or guardian must agree before a child uses Test Series. We record their email, and the date and version of their consent. [[As the DPDP Rules phase in (the child-consent rules apply from 13 May 2027), we will add a stronger way to verify that the person consenting is the child’s parent or guardian, and will update this notice.]]
  • We do not track or monitor children’s behaviour, build profiles of them, or show them targeted advertising. There are no ads on Physica.
  • Visit statistics are off by default and we ask you to opt in only if you are 18 or older. Analytics are switched off whenever Test Series is open.
  • We collect only what is listed in section 3 and nothing that could harm a child’s well-being.
  • A parent or guardian may ask to see, correct or delete their child’s data, or withdraw consent, by emailing easy@physica.in from the address on file or through the child’s account.

6. Cookies, local storage and visit statistics

Physica sets no cookies of its own. It uses your browser’s local/session storage for the settings in section 2 and the sign-in token in section 3.

Anonymous visit counts (always on). To know how many people use Physica and which simulations are popular, your browser sends us one tiny message when you open the lab (“a visit”) and when you open a simulation (its name, for example “projectile-motion”), and we add 1 to that day’s total. That is all: no cookie, no ID, no IP address stored by us, no record per visitor, so nobody can be identified or followed from it, and it is not linked to any account. It is not sent if your browser sends a Do‑Not‑Track or Global Privacy Control signal. (Like any website, our hosting providers’ servers see your IP address briefly in their technical logs when a page loads.)

7. How we protect data

No system can be made perfectly secure, but we design Test Series so that a mistake in one place does not expose everything. Among other things:

  • All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS); data at rest is encrypted by our database provider.
  • Sign-in uses one-time codes by email, so there are no passwords for us to lose.
  • Test answers, solutions and other students’ data cannot be read directly from the browser. Every request is checked on the server against who you are, and answers are revealed only after you submit.
  • Strict input checks, rate limits against automated abuse, and an audit log of sensitive actions.
  • Administrator access needs a second verification step (multi-factor).
  • New-device alerts, a “log out on all devices” button, and the ability for us to lock an account or pause the whole service within minutes if we suspect misuse. A locked account can still download or delete its data.
  • A watchdog that checks every 30 minutes that the live website is exactly what we published.
  • Strict browser security rules (Content Security Policy) and no third-party scripts inside Test Series.
  • Regular automated security tests, and limited access to data on a need-to-know basis.

If something goes wrong. If a personal-data breach affects you we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires, and report cyber incidents to CERT-In within the time the law sets (currently 6 hours). To report a security problem, write to easy@physica.in (see security.txt). Please give us reasonable time to fix it before you make it public; we will not take action against good-faith research.

8. How long we keep data

Deleted data may remain in our database provider’s encrypted backups for up to [[BACKUP_DAYS, e.g. 7]] days before it is overwritten.   • Withdraw consent at any time (by deleting your account).
