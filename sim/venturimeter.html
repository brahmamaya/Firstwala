Venturimeter

Water speeds up through a narrow throat and its pressure drops — read the drop on the vertical tubes.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Fluid moves fastest where the pipe is narrowest, and the pressure there is lowest.

Key relation

A₁v₁ = A₂v₂ ; P₁ − P₂ = ½ρ(v₂² − v₁²)

Try this

Halve the throat diameter. By what factor does the speed rise?

What you can change

  • Flow rate: 0.5 to 10 L/s
  • Main pipe diameter: 4 to 10 cm
  • Throat diameter: 1.5 to 4 cm
