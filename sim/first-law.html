First law of thermodynamics

Add heat and let the gas do work; track its internal energy.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Energy is conserved: heat in either warms the gas or is spent doing work.

Key relation

ΔU = Q − W

Try this

Add heat while the gas does an equal amount of work — ΔU stays zero.

What you can change

  • Heat added Q: -100 to 200 J
  • Work by gas W: -100 to 200 J
