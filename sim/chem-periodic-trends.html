What to notice

Noble gases have the highest ionisation enthalpies; alkali metals the lowest. Radius shrinks across a period as nuclear charge rises.

Key relation

Across a period: radius ↓, ionisation enthalpy ↑, electronegativity ↑ ; down a group: the reverse
