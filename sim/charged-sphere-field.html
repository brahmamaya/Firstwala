Field of a charged sphere

Compare a conducting shell with a uniformly charged insulating sphere, inside and outside.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Outside, both look like a point charge at the centre; inside, the conductor shields completely.

Key relation

Outside: E = kQ/r² ; inside conductor: 0 ; inside insulator: kQr/R³

Try this

Move the probe inside the sphere and switch between the two types.

What you can change

  • Sphere: choose Conducting shell, Uniformly charged insulator
  • Charge Q: 1 to 50 nC
  • Sphere radius R: 2 to 20 cm
  • Probe distance r: 0 to 40 cm
