Transcription and translation

Transcribe a short gene into mRNA and translate it codon by codon. Then make a point mutation and see its effect.

Open the simulation

What to notice

A single base change can be silent, change one amino acid (missense) or create a stop codon (nonsense).

Key relation

DNA → mRNA (transcription) → protein (translation) ; AUG = start (Met)

Try this

Change codon 3 (AAA) to a stop codon by mutating one base.

What you can change

  • Mutation position (0 = none): 0 to 18
  • New base: choose A, T, G, C
