Magnetic dipole: axis and equator

Move away from a small bar magnet and compare its axial and equatorial fields.

Open the simulation

What to notice

At equal distances, the axial field magnitude is twice the equatorial field.

Key relation

Baxis = 2μ₀m/(4πr³); Beq = μ₀m/(4πr³)

Try this

Double the distance and watch both fields fall by a factor of eight.

What you can change

  • Magnetic moment: 0.5 to 10 A·m²
  • Distance from centre: 0.2 to 2 m
