Resolving two stars (Rayleigh criterion)

Two point sources make overlapping diffraction patterns. Are they resolved by this aperture?

Open the simulation

What to notice

When the peak of one pattern falls on the first dark ring of the other, the sources are just resolved.

Key relation

θ_min = 1.22 λ / D

Try this

Increase the aperture diameter until the two peaks separate.

What you can change

  • Aperture diameter: 1 to 200 mm
  • Wavelength: 400 to 700 nm
  • Source separation: 0.5 to 20 arcsec
