Decomposition of detritus

Watch leaf litter break down. Warmth, moisture and nitrogen-rich litter speed it up; lignin and chitin slow it.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Low temperature and anaerobic conditions inhibit decomposition, so organic matter builds up.

Key relation

Fragmentation → leaching → catabolism → humification → mineralisation

Try this

Make the litter lignin-rich and the soil cold. How much remains after a year?

What you can change

  • Temperature: 0 to 40 °C
  • Moisture: 0 to 100 %
  • Lignin / chitin content: 0 to 60 %
  • Time: 0 to 24 months
