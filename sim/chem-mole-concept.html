Mole concept: mass, moles and particles

Weigh out a substance and convert its mass into moles and into the number of molecules.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Equal numbers of moles contain equal numbers of particles, whatever their masses.

Key relation

n = m / M ; N = n × Nₐ (Nₐ = 6.022 × 10²³ mol⁻¹)

Try this

Take 18 g of water and then 44 g of CO₂. How many molecules does each contain?
