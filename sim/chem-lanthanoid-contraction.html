Lanthanoid contraction

Step across the lanthanoids and see the steady decrease in the size of Ln³⁺ ions.

Open the simulation

What to notice

The radius falls from La³⁺ (103 pm) to Lu³⁺ (86 pm); as a result Zr and Hf of the next row are almost the same size.

Key relation

4f electrons shield poorly → effective nuclear charge rises → ions shrink
