Bimetallic strip

Bond two metals and heat them. The one that expands more ends up on the outside of the curve.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Heating curls the strip towards the metal with the smaller expansion coefficient; cooling reverses it.

Key relation

1/ρ ≈ 3(α₂ − α₁)ΔT / 2h

Try this

Make ΔT negative. Which way does the strip bend now?

What you can change

  • Metal pair: choose Brass / steel, Copper / invar, Aluminium / steel
  • Temperature change ΔT: -100 to 200 K
  • Total thickness: 0.5 to 3 mm
  • Length: 5 to 20 cm
