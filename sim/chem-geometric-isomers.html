Geometrical isomers of complexes

Compare cis and trans [Co(NH₃)₄Cl₂]⁺ and fac and mer [Co(NH₃)₃Cl₃].

Open the simulation

What to notice

cis-[Co(NH₃)₄Cl₂]⁺ is violet and trans is green: the same formula, different arrangement and properties.

Key relation

cis: same ligands at 90° ; trans: at 180° ; fac: three on one face ; mer: three around a meridian
