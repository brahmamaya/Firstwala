Electron beam between deflecting plates

Accelerate electrons, then pass them between charged plates. The beam bends into a parabola and hits the screen.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Inside the plates the path is a parabola; after leaving, the electron travels in a straight line.

Key relation

y = V_d L² / (4 d V_a) ; Y = (V_d L / 2dV_a)(L/2 + D)

Try this

Double the accelerating voltage. How does the spot move?

What you can change

  • Accelerating voltage: 500 to 5000 V
  • Deflecting voltage: 0 to 200 V
  • Plate length: 2 to 6 cm
  • Plate separation: 0.5 to 2 cm
  • Plates to screen: 10 to 30 cm
