What to notice

No charge crosses the vacuum gap. A changing electric field supplies the displacement-current term.

Try this

Pause at maximum field: the instantaneous current should be zero.

What you can change

  • Peak charging current: 1 to 10 nA
  • Charge-cycle frequency: 0.5 to 2 Hz
  • Plate area: 0.01 to 0.08 m²
