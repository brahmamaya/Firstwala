Le Chatelier’s principle (Haber process)

Disturb N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃ by changing pressure, temperature or adding reactant and watch the equilibrium shift.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Higher pressure and lower temperature favour ammonia; adding N₂ also shifts the equilibrium to the right.

Key relation

N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃ ; ΔH = −92.4 kJ/mol

Try this

Raise the temperature from 500 K to 800 K. Does the yield of NH₃ rise or fall?

What you can change

  • Pressure: 50 to 300 atm
  • Temperature: 500 to 900 K
  • Extra N₂ added: 0 to 2 ×
