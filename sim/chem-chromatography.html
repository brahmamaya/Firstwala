Paper chromatography and Rf

Run a spot of leaf pigment extract up a strip of paper and measure each component’s retardation factor.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Components that are less strongly held by the paper (stationary phase) travel further and have higher R_f.

Key relation

R_f = distance moved by the substance / distance moved by the solvent front

Try this

Stop the run when the solvent has moved 6 cm and calculate R_f of chlorophyll a.

What you can change

  • Solvent front distance: 0 to 10 cm
