What to notice

A closed pipe has a node at the closed end and supports only odd harmonics.

Key relation

Open: fₙ = nv/2L ; Closed: fₙ = (2n − 1)v/4L

Compare the fundamental of an open and a closed pipe of the same length.

What you can change

  • Pipe: choose Open at both ends, Closed at one end
  • Pipe length: 0.2 to 2 m
  • Harmonic number n: 1 to 5
  • Air temperature: 0 to 40 °C
