Oscillating liquid in a U-tube

Displace the liquid in a U-tube and release it. The column oscillates in simple harmonic motion.

Open the simulation

What to notice

The period depends only on the total length of the liquid column, not on its density.

Key relation

T = 2π √(L / 2g)

Try this

Quadruple the column length. Does the period double?

What you can change

  • Liquid column length: 0.2 to 2 m
  • Initial displacement: 0.01 to 0.1 m
