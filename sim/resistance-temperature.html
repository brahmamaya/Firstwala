Resistance and temperature

Heat a resistor and watch its resistance change. Metals rise; carbon falls.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Metals have positive α because lattice vibrations scatter electrons more at higher temperature.

Key relation

R = R₀[1 + α(T − T₀)]

Try this

Compare nichrome with copper: which makes a better standard resistor?

What you can change

  • Material: choose Copper (α = 3.9 × 10⁻³ /K), Tungsten (α = 4.5 × 10⁻³ /K), Nichrome (α = 0.4 × 10⁻³ /K), Carbon (α = −0.5 × 10⁻³ /K)
  • Resistance at 20 °C: 1 to 100 Ω
  • Temperature: -50 to 600 °C
