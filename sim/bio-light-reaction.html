Light reaction: the Z scheme

Follow electrons from water through PS II and PS I to NADP⁺ in the thylakoid membrane, and protons through ATP synthase.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Splitting water releases O₂ and supplies electrons to PS II; the proton gradient across the thylakoid drives ATP synthesis.

Key relation

2H₂O → 4H⁺ + O₂ + 4e⁻ ; NADP⁺ + 2e⁻ + H⁺ → NADPH
