Projectile with air resistance

Add linear air drag and compare the path with the ideal vacuum parabola.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Drag shortens the range and makes the descent steeper than the ascent.

Key relation

x = (u_x/k)(1 − e^(−kt)), y = (u_y/k + g/k²)(1 − e^(−kt)) − gt/k
