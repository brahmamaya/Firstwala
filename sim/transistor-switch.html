Transistor as a switch

Drive a transistor through a base resistor and see when a collector load is fully switched on.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Once saturated, extra base current does not significantly increase load current.

Key relation

IC ≈ min(βIB, (VCC − Vsat)/RC)

Try this

Lower the base resistance until the transistor saturates.

What you can change

  • Input voltage: 0 to 5 V
  • Base resistance: 1 to 100 kΩ
  • Current gain β: 20 to 200
