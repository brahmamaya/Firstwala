Galvanometer to ammeter or voltmeter

Add a small shunt for an ammeter or a large series resistance for a voltmeter.

Open the simulation

What to notice

An ammeter needs a very low resistance; a voltmeter needs a very high one.

Key relation

Ammeter: S = I_gG / (I − I_g) ; Voltmeter: R = V/I_g − G

Try this

Convert to a 10 A ammeter, then to a 100 V voltmeter.

What you can change

  • Convert to: choose Ammeter (shunt), Voltmeter (series R)
  • Galvanometer resistance G: 10 to 200 Ω
  • Full-scale current I_g: 1 to 10 mA
  • Desired range: 1 to 100 A or V
