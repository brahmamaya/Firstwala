Cells in series and parallel

Combine n identical cells in series or in parallel and compare the current through a load.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Series helps when the load resistance is large; parallel helps when it is small compared with r.

Key relation

Series: I = nE/(R + nr) ; Parallel: I = E/(R + r/n)

Try this

Set R much smaller than r. Which arrangement gives more current?

What you can change

  • Arrangement: choose Series, Parallel
  • Number of cells n: 1 to 8
  • EMF of each cell: 1 to 2 V
  • Internal resistance: 0.1 to 2 Ω
  • Load resistance R: 0.5 to 20 Ω
