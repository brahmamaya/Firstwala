Block on an incline with a hanging mass

A block on a slope is tied over a spoked pulley to a hanging mass. Choose the masses, the angle and the friction, press Run and watch the real motion with all the forces.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Friction always opposes the motion; if it can balance the pull, nothing moves. The rope tension is the same on both sides of a light, smooth pulley.

Key relation

a = (m₂g − m₁g sinθ ∓ μm₁g cosθ) / (m₁ + m₂) ; T = m₂(g − a)

Try this

Find the hanging mass that just starts the block moving up the slope. Then add friction and run again.

What you can change

  • Block on incline m₁: 1 to 10 kg
  • Hanging mass m₂: 0.5 to 10 kg
  • Incline angle θ: 10 to 60 °
  • Friction coefficient μ: 0 to 0.8
