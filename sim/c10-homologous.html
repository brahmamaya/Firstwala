Homologous series of carbon compounds

Build members of a homologous series one carbon at a time and see the formula, name, molar mass and boiling point change.

Open the simulation

What to notice

All members share a general formula and the same functional group, so their chemical properties are alike, while the boiling point rises steadily with size.

Key relation

Successive members differ by –CH₂– (14 u)

Try this

Compare ethanol with propanol, and with ethanoic acid. What stays the same and what changes?

What you can change
