Transmission across a chemical synapse

An impulse arrives at the axon terminal: synaptic vesicles release neurotransmitter, which crosses the cleft and binds receptors.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Chemical synapses are slower than electrical synapses because the transmitter must diffuse across the cleft.

Key relation

Electrical signal → chemical (neurotransmitter) → electrical signal
