Drawing water from a well (fixed pulley)

A boy or a girl (you choose, and you can type the name) pulls a rope over a pulley on a wooden frame and lifts a bucket of water out of the well. Change the water and his pull and watch the bucket speed up, rise at constant speed or fall back.

Open the simulation

What to notice

A fixed pulley only changes the direction of the force: the pull equals the weight. The person can pull down with at most their own weight, or they would be lifted off the ground.

Key relation

a = (F − mg)/(m + I/r²) ; at constant speed F = mg ; work by the boy = mgh + ½mv²

Try this

Fill the bucket with 10 litres. Find the pull that lifts it at constant speed. Then pull 30 N harder and see the acceleration.

What you can change

  • Who is pulling?: choose Boy, Girl
  • Water in the bucket: 0 to 15 L
  • Pull on the rope: 0 to 600 N
  • Mass of the person: 30 to 70 kg
