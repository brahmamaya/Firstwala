Pressure inside drops and bubbles

Surface tension squeezes a drop. A soap bubble has two surfaces, so its excess pressure doubles.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Smaller drops and bubbles have a higher internal pressure.

Key relation

Drop: ΔP = 2T/r ; Bubble: ΔP = 4T/r

Try this

Make the radius ten times smaller.

What you can change

  • Object: choose Liquid drop, Soap bubble, Air bubble inside liquid
  • Radius: 0.1 to 50 mm
  • Surface tension: 0.02 to 0.08 N/m
