Self-inductance of a solenoid

Change the turns, size and core of a solenoid and find its inductance and stored energy.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Inductance grows with the square of the number of turns; an iron core multiplies it greatly.

Key relation

L = μ₀μᵣN²A / l ; U = ½LI²

Try this

Double the turns, then insert the iron core.

What you can change

  • Turns N: 50 to 2000
  • Length l: 5 to 50 cm
  • Radius r: 0.5 to 5 cm
  • Core μᵣ: 1 to 1000
  • Current I: 0.1 to 5 A
