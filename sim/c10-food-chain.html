Food chain: energy flow and biological magnification

Follow energy and a non-biodegradable pesticide up a food chain.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Energy shrinks at every step, so food chains are short; a pesticide like DDT is not broken down and builds up, so top consumers carry the most.

Key relation

Energy passed on ≈ 10% per level; pesticide concentration × (magnification) per level

Try this

Make the pesticide in the water only 0.02 ppm. How much reaches the top consumer?

What you can change

  • Energy fixed by producers: 1000 to 100000 J
  • Pesticide in the water: 0.01 to 1 ppm
  • Build-up factor per level: 2 to 20 ×
