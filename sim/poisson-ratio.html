Poisson’s ratio: stretch and thin

Stretch a wire and watch it get thinner. The lateral strain is a fixed fraction of the longitudinal strain.

Open the simulation

What to notice

With σ = 0.5 the volume is unchanged (like rubber); most metals have σ ≈ 0.3.

Key relation

σ = −(Δd/d) / (ΔL/L), ΔV/V = ε(1 − 2σ)

Set σ = 0.5. What happens to the volume change?

What you can change

  • Tension F: 0 to 2000 N
  • Wire diameter: 0.5 to 3 mm
  • Young’s modulus: 50 to 210 GPa
  • Poisson’s ratio σ: 0 to 0.5
