Thermal stress in a clamped rod

Heat a rod held between rigid walls. It cannot expand, so a large compressive stress builds up.

Open the simulation

What to notice

The stress does not depend on the rod’s length — only on the material and the temperature rise.

Key relation

stress = Y α ΔT ; F = Y A α ΔT

Try this

Compare steel with aluminium for the same temperature rise.

What you can change

  • Material: choose Steel, Copper, Aluminium
  • Temperature rise ΔT: 0 to 100 K
  • Cross-section area: 1 to 10 cm²
