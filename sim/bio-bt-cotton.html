Bt cotton: how the Bt toxin works

Bollworm larvae feed on Bt and non-Bt cotton. In the alkaline gut the inactive protoxin becomes an active toxin.

Open the simulation

What to notice

The toxin does not harm the bacterium itself, because it is stored as an inactive protoxin.

Key relation

Inactive protoxin —alkaline gut pH→ active toxin → pores in midgut cells → death

Try this

Compare larva survival on Bt and non-Bt plants after a week.

What you can change

  • Plant: choose Bt cotton (cry genes), Non-Bt cotton
  • Days of feeding: 0 to 10 days
  • Larvae on plant: 2 to 12
