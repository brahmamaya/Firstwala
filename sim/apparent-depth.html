Real and apparent depth

Look straight down into a pool. Refraction makes the coin on the bottom appear raised.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Light bends away from the normal as it leaves the water, so the eye traces it back to a shallower point.

Key relation

apparent depth = real depth / n ; shift = d(1 − 1/n)

Try this

Try glass (1.5) and diamond (2.42) with the same depth.

What you can change

  • Real depth: 0.2 to 3 m
  • Refractive index: 1 to 2.42
