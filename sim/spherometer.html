Spherometer

Lower the central screw onto a curved lens and calculate its radius of curvature from the sagitta.

Open the simulation

What to notice

The three legs form an equilateral triangle; the central screw measures how far the curved surface rises (h).

Key relation

R = l² / 6h + h / 2

Try this

Keep the leg spacing fixed and halve h. Does R double?

What you can change

  • Complete screw turns: 0 to 4
  • Circular-scale division: 0 to 99
  • Leg spacing l: 20 to 60 mm
