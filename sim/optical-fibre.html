Guiding light in an optical fibre

Launch a ray inside a glass core and check whether the surrounding layer keeps it trapped.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Total internal reflection needs a higher-index core and incidence above the critical angle at each wall.

Key relation

θcritical = sin⁻¹(ncladding / ncore)

Try this

Increase the internal launch angle until the ray leaks out.

What you can change

  • Core refractive index: 1.45 to 1.65
  • Cladding refractive index: 1.3 to 1.44
  • Ray angle to fibre axis: 5 to 45 °
