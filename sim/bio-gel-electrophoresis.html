Agarose gel electrophoresis

Load DNA fragments into wells and run the gel. Negatively charged DNA moves towards the anode; smaller fragments move farther.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Bands are seen after staining with ethidium bromide and UV light; they can be cut out (elution) and used for cloning.

Key relation

Distance moved ∝ log of (1 / fragment size), roughly

Try this

Increase the run time. Do small fragments run off the gel first?

What you can change

  • Run time: 0 to 60 min
  • Voltage: 50 to 150 V
  • Your sample: fragment size: 200 to 9000 bp
