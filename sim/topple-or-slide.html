Topple or slide?

Push a tall block with a growing force. It either slides first or tips over its front edge first.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Pushing higher or using a narrower block makes tipping happen before sliding.

Key relation

Slides at F = μmg ; tips at F = mg (b/2) / h_F

Try this

Push at the top of a tall, narrow block on a high-friction floor.

What you can change

  • Block width: 0.2 to 1 m
  • Block height: 0.4 to 2 m
  • Push height (fraction of H): 0.1 to 1
  • Floor friction μ: 0.1 to 1
