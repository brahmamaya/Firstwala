Moving-coil galvanometer

Current in a coil within a radial magnetic field produces a torque balanced by a spring.

Open the simulation

What to notice

The radial field keeps the torque proportional to current, so the scale is linear.

Key relation

θ = NABI / k

Try this

Double the number of turns. How does the current sensitivity change?

What you can change

  • Current I: 0 to 100 μA
  • Turns N: 10 to 500
  • Coil area: 1 to 10 cm²
  • Magnetic field: 0.05 to 0.5 T
  • Spring constant k: 0.1 to 5 μN m/rad
