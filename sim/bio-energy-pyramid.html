Pyramid of energy (10 per cent law)

Pass energy up the food chain. Only about 10% reaches the next trophic level.

Open the simulation

What to notice

A pyramid of energy is always upright — energy is lost as heat at every step.

Key relation

Energy at level n = E₁ × (efficiency)ⁿ⁻¹
