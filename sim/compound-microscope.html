Compound microscope

An objective makes a magnified real image; the eyepiece magnifies it further.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Short focal lengths for both lenses and a longer tube give greater magnification.

Key relation

m = (L/f_o)(1 + D/f_e) or (L/f_o)(D/f_e)

Try this

Halve the objective focal length. Compare the change with halving f_e.

What you can change

  • Objective focal length f_o: 0.4 to 2 cm
  • Eyepiece focal length f_e: 1.5 to 6 cm
  • Tube length L: 10 to 20 cm
  • Final image: choose At near point (25 cm), At infinity (relaxed eye)
