Partially filled capacitor

Slide a dielectric slab of thickness t between the plates and see the capacitance rise.

Open the simulation

What to notice

The slab acts like a thinner air gap: each millimetre of dielectric counts as only 1/κ mm.

Key relation

C = ε₀A / (d − t + t/κ)

Try this

Set t = d. Compare with κε₀A/d.

What you can change

  • Plate area: 50 to 500 cm²
  • Plate separation d: 1 to 10 mm
  • Slab thickness t: 0 to 10 mm
  • Dielectric constant κ: 1 to 10
