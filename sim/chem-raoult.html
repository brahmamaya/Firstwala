Raoult’s law: vapour pressure of a mixture

Mix benzene and toluene and see how the total and partial vapour pressures depend on the mole fraction.

Open the simulation

What to notice

For an ideal solution the total vapour pressure varies linearly between the two pure liquids.

Key relation

p_A = x_A p°_A ; p_total = x_A p°_A + x_B p°_B
