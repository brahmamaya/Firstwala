What to notice

Adding Cl⁻ from NaCl shifts the equilibrium left, so much less AgCl dissolves.

Key relation

AgCl ⇌ Ag⁺ + Cl⁻ ; K_sp = [Ag⁺][Cl⁻] = 1.8 × 10⁻¹⁰ (298 K)
