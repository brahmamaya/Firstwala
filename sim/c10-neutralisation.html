Neutralisation: acid + base → salt + water

Add sodium hydroxide from a burette to 25 mL of hydrochloric acid with phenolphthalein, and follow the pH.

Open the simulation

What to notice

The pH rises slowly, jumps sharply near 25 mL (neutral point) and the solution turns pink once it is basic.

Key relation

HCl + NaOH → NaCl + H₂O

Find the exact volume of NaOH at which the pink colour first appears.
