LED colour and band gap

Change the semiconductor band gap and see the colour of light the LED emits.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Wider band gaps release more energetic photons — towards blue and violet.

Key relation

λ = hc / E_g ≈ 1240 nm·eV / E_g

Try this

Find the band gap that gives red light (≈ 630 nm).

What you can change

  • Band gap E_g: 1.4 to 3.3 eV
  • Forward current: 0 to 30 mA
