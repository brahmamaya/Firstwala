Double circulation

Follow red blood cells through the pulmonary circuit (heart–lungs) and the systemic circuit (heart–body).

Open the simulation

What to notice

Oxygenated and deoxygenated blood never mix in the human four-chambered heart.

Key relation

Right ventricle → lungs → left atrium ; left ventricle → body → right atrium
