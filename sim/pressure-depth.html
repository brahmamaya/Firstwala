Pressure at depth in a tank

Lower a pressure probe into a tank and change the liquid. Find the force on one side wall too.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Pressure depends only on depth and density — not on the tank’s shape or the amount of liquid.

Key relation

P = P₀ + ρgh ; F_wall = ½ρgH²W

Try this

Switch to mercury and keep the probe depth fixed.

What you can change

  • Liquid: choose Water (1000 kg/m³), Oil (800 kg/m³), Mercury (13 600 kg/m³)
  • Liquid depth H: 0.5 to 5 m
  • Probe depth (fraction of H): 0 to 1
  • Wall width W: 1 to 5 m
