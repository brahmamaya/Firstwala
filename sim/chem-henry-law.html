Henry’s law: gas dissolved in a liquid

Raise the partial pressure of a gas above water and see how much dissolves.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Solubility is proportional to pressure; gases with smaller K_H (like CO₂) dissolve far more. That is why soft drinks are bottled under CO₂ pressure.

Key relation

p = K_H x (x = mole fraction of gas in solution)
