Electric field on a ring axis

Move a probe along the axis of a uniformly charged ring and locate the strongest field.

Open the simulation

What to notice

The field is zero at the centre and peaks at x = R/√2.

Key relation

E = kQx/(x² + R²)^(3/2)

Try this

Move the probe to about 0.71 times the ring radius.

What you can change

  • Ring charge: 1 to 10 nC
  • Ring radius: 0.1 to 1 m
  • Axial position: -2 to 2 m
