Pick a compound and see how the oxidation number of the central element is found from the rules.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Oxidation numbers can be fractional as averages (Fe₃O₄ gives +8/3); in H₂O₂ oxygen is −1 and in OF₂ it is +2.

Key relation

Sum of oxidation numbers = charge ; H = +1, O = −2 (except peroxides and OF₂), alkali metals = +1, F = −1
