Lift on an aerofoil

Air flows faster over the curved top of a wing. Bernoulli’s principle gives the pressure difference.

Open the simulation

What to notice

A small speed difference over a large wing area produces enough lift to hold up an aircraft.

Key relation

L = ½ρ(v_top² − v_bottom²) A

Try this

Halve the air density (high altitude). How much faster must the plane fly?

What you can change

  • Airspeed under wing: 50 to 250 m/s
  • Speed ratio top/bottom: 1 to 1.3
  • Wing area: 10 to 120 m²
  • Air density: 0.4 to 1.25 kg/m³
