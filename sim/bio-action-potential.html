Nerve impulse: the action potential

Stimulate an axon. If the stimulus reaches threshold, Na⁺ rushes in and a full action potential travels along it.

Open the simulation

What to notice

The response is all-or-none: below threshold nothing travels; above it the spike is always the same size.

Key relation

Resting ≈ −70 mV → depolarisation (Na⁺ in) → repolarisation (K⁺ out)

Try this

Increase the stimulus slowly. At what value does the impulse fire?

What you can change

  • Stimulus strength: 0 to 40 mV
  • Threshold depolarisation: 10 to 25 mV
