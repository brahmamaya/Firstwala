Area under a v–t graph

Read displacement as the area beneath a velocity–time graph.

Open the simulation

What to notice

A constant velocity gives a rectangle; steady acceleration adds a triangle.

Key relation

displacement = area under v–t

Try this

Set acceleration to zero and read the rectangular area.

What you can change

  • Initial velocity: 0 to 20 m/s
  • Acceleration: -3 to 4 m/s²
  • Duration: 2 to 10 s
