Acidity of phenols

Compare the pKₐ of ethanol, phenol and substituted phenols.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Phenol is far more acidic than ethanol; nitro groups at ortho and para positions increase acidity more than at meta; picric acid is almost as strong as a mineral acid.

Key relation

smaller pKₐ = stronger acid ; –NO₂ at o/p stabilises phenoxide by resonance
