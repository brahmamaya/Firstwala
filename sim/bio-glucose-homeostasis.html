Insulin, glucagon and blood glucose

Eat a meal and watch insulin from β-cells bring blood glucose back down; glucagon from α-cells raises it when low.

Open the simulation

What to notice

In diabetes mellitus glucose stays high for long because insulin is lacking or ineffective.

Key relation

Insulin: hypoglycaemic ; glucagon: hyperglycaemic

Try this

Compare the normal and diabetic curves after the same meal.

What you can change

  • Carbohydrate in meal: 0 to 150 g
  • Time after meal: 0 to 240 min
  • Condition: choose Normal, Diabetes mellitus
