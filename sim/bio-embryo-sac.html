What to notice

At the micropylar end: egg apparatus (2 synergids + egg); at the chalazal end: 3 antipodals; centre: 2 polar nuclei.

Key relation

1 functional megaspore → 3 mitoses → 8 nuclei → 7 cells (monosporic development)
