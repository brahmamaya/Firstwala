Photoperiodism and flowering

Set the day length and see whether a short-day, long-day or day-neutral plant flowers.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Leaves perceive the photoperiod; the flowering signal travels to the shoot apex.

Key relation

Short-day: flowers if day < critical length ; long-day: if day > critical length

Try this

Set the critical length to 12 h and slowly change the day length for each plant type.

What you can change

  • Plant type: choose Short-day plant, Long-day plant, Day-neutral plant
  • Day length: 6 to 18 h
  • Critical day length: 10 to 14 h
