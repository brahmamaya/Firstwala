Zener voltage regulator

Vary the input and load, and discover when a reverse-biased Zener diode can hold the output steady.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Regulation holds only while the series resistor supplies enough current for both the load and the Zener.

Key relation

Iseries = Iload + IZ

Try this

Use the dropout preset, then reduce the series resistance.

What you can change

  • Input voltage: 6 to 18 V
  • Series resistance: 100 to 1000 Ω
  • Load resistance: 500 to 3000 Ω
  • Zener voltage: choose 3.3 V, 5.1 V, 6.8 V
