Electrocardiogram (ECG)

Read an ECG trace: P wave, QRS complex and T wave. Count QRS complexes to find the heart rate.

Open the simulation

What to notice

P: atrial depolarisation; QRS: ventricular depolarisation (start of contraction); T: ventricular repolarisation.

Key relation

Heart rate = 60 / (R–R interval in s)

Try this

Raise the heart rate and measure the shorter R–R interval.

What you can change

  • Heart rate: 40 to 180 beats/min
  • Highlight wave: choose P wave, QRS complex, T wave
