Viscous drag and terminal speed

Release a small sphere in thick liquid and watch drag grow until the forces balance.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Weight is downward. Buoyancy and viscous drag act upward, reducing the acceleration as speed rises.

Key relation

vterminal = 2r²(ρs − ρf)g/(9η)

Try this

Double the sphere radius and compare the terminal speed.

What you can change

  • Sphere radius: 0.5 to 2 mm
  • Dynamic viscosity: 2 to 10 Pa·s
  • Sphere density: 2000 to 8000 kg/m³
