Benzene: resonance and equal bonds

Flip between the two Kekulé structures and the resonance hybrid of benzene.

Open the simulation

What to notice

All six C–C bonds in benzene are equal (139 pm): the π electrons are delocalised over the ring.

Key relation

C–C (single) 154 pm, C=C (double) 134 pm, benzene C–C 139 pm
