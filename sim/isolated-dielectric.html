What to notice

Inserting a dielectric reduces both voltage and stored field energy when charge is fixed.

Key relation

Q constant; V = Q/C; U = Q²/(2C)

What you can change

  • Dielectric constant: 1 to 8
  • Inserted area: 0 to 100 %
