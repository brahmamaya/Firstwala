Spermatogenesis vs oogenesis

Compare how many gametes form from one primary spermatocyte and one primary oocyte.

What to notice

Meiosis halves the chromosome number from 46 to 23; in oogenesis unequal division conserves cytoplasm in the ovum.

Key relation

Primary spermatocyte → 4 sperms ; primary oocyte → 1 ovum + polar bodies
