Heating gas at constant pressure

Weights hold the piston pressure fixed while you heat the gas. Track Q, W and ΔU.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Only part of the heat raises internal energy; the rest does work lifting the piston.

Key relation

W = nRΔT, Q = nC_pΔT, ΔU = nC_vΔT

Try this

Switch to a diatomic gas. What fraction of Q becomes work now?

What you can change

  • Amount of gas: 0.1 to 2 mol
  • Pressure: 50 to 300 kPa
  • Initial temperature: 250 to 400 K
  • Final temperature: 300 to 800 K
  • Gas: choose Monatomic (Cv = 3R/2), Diatomic (Cv = 5R/2)
