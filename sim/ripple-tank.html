Ripple tank: two-source interference

Two dippers make circular waves on water. Watch lines of calm water appear where the waves cancel.

Open the simulation

What to notice

The pattern of calm lines (nodal lines) spreads out when the sources are closer or the wavelength is longer.

Key relation

Path difference = nλ → bright ; (n + ½)λ → dark

Try this

Double the source separation. Count the nodal lines.

What you can change

  • Source separation d: 0.5 to 3 units
  • Wavelength: 0.3 to 1.2 units
  • Wave height: 0.05 to 0.3
