Activity of a radioactive sample

Pick an isotope and a sample mass. Count the atoms and calculate how many decay each second.

Open the simulation

What to notice

A milligram of a short-lived isotope can be far more active than a kilogram of a long-lived one.

Key relation

A = λN = (ln 2 / T½) × (m/M) N_A

Try this

Compare 1 mg of iodine-131 with 1 mg of carbon-14.

What you can change

  • Isotope: choose Iodine-131 (8.02 d), Cobalt-60 (5.27 y), Caesium-137 (30.1 y), Radium-226 (1600 y), Carbon-14 (5730 y)
  • Sample mass (log₁₀ mg): -3 to 3
  • Time elapsed: 0 to 100 years
