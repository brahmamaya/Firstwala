Radiation from a dipole antenna

A half-wave dipole radiates most strongly at right angles to its length and not at all along it.

Open the simulation

What to notice

The 3D radiation pattern is a doughnut wrapped around the antenna.

Key relation

I(θ) ∝ sin²θ ; f = c / 2L

Try this

Move the receiver angle towards 0°. What happens to the signal?

What you can change

  • Antenna length L: 0.1 to 3 m
  • Receiver angle θ: 0 to 90 °
