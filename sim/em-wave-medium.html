What to notice

Frequency stays the same in the medium; speed and wavelength both drop by a factor n.

Key relation

v = c/n, λ = λ₀/n, B₀ = nE₀/c

Try this

Increase n. Watch the wave crests bunch up inside the medium.

What you can change

  • Refractive index n: 1 to 2.5
  • Vacuum wavelength: 400 to 700 nm
  • Polarisation angle: 0 to 90 °
  • Field amplitude E₀: 10 to 1000 V/m
