Incomplete dominance and co-dominance

Cross snapdragons (incomplete dominance) or combine ABO alleles (co-dominance) and compare the offspring.

Open the simulation

What to notice

In incomplete dominance the F₂ phenotypic ratio equals the genotypic ratio, 1 : 2 : 1.

Key relation

Snapdragon: RR (red) × rr (white) → Rr (pink) ; ABO: Iᴬ Iᴮ → group AB

Try this

Self the pink F₁ (Rr × Rr). What fraction of flowers are pink?

What you can change

  • Parent 2: choose RR / IᴮIᴮ, Rr / IᴬIᴮ, rr / ii
