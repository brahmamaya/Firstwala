pH of acids and bases

Choose an acid or base and its concentration; see the pH, the indicator colour and the ions in solution.

Open the simulation

What to notice

A tenfold dilution of a strong acid raises pH by one unit; a weak acid at the same concentration has a higher pH.

Key relation

pH = −log[H₃O⁺] ; weak acid: [H₃O⁺] ≈ √(K_a c) ; pH + pOH = 14 (298 K)
