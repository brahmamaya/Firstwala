Total internal reflection

Shine a laser from glass towards air. Beyond the critical angle no light escapes.

Open the simulation

What to notice

As the angle grows the refracted ray skims the surface, then vanishes — all light is reflected.

Key relation

sin θc = n₂ / n₁

Try this

Set the angle just above θc. Then switch to a diamond-like index (2.42).

What you can change

  • Index of dense medium n₁: 1.3 to 2.42
  • Index of outside medium n₂: 1 to 1.5
  • Angle of incidence: 0 to 89 °
