Dicot vs monocot stem

Compare transverse sections of dicot and monocot stems: arrangement and type of vascular bundles.

What to notice

Dicot bundles lie in a ring and contain cambium; monocot bundles are scattered and closed.

Key relation

Open bundles (with cambium) → secondary growth possible
