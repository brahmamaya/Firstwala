Conformations of ethane

Rotate one carbon of ethane about the C–C bond and follow the torsional energy between staggered and eclipsed forms.

Open the simulation

What to notice

The staggered form (φ = 60°) is the most stable; the eclipsed form (φ = 0°) is 12.5 kJ/mol higher, but rotation is still rapid at room temperature.

Key relation

E(φ) ≈ ½ E₀ (1 + cos 3φ) ; E₀ ≈ 12.5 kJ/mol
