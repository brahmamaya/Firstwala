Basic strength of amines

Compare the pK_b of ammonia, aliphatic amines and aniline in water.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Alkyl groups push electrons to N, making aliphatic amines stronger bases than ammonia; in water, solvation makes 2° amines strongest. Aniline is much weaker because its lone pair is delocalised into the ring.

Key relation

pK_b = −log K_b ; smaller pK_b = stronger base
