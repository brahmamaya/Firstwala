Reflex arc: the knee jerk

Tap the patellar tendon. The impulse runs in through a sensory neuron and straight out through a motor neuron in the spinal cord.

Open the simulation

What to notice

The reflex happens involuntarily, without waiting for a decision from the brain.

Key relation

Receptor → afferent neuron → spinal cord → efferent neuron → effector
