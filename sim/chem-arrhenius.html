Arrhenius equation and activation energy

Change the temperature and activation energy and see how many molecules have enough energy to react.

What to notice

A 10 K rise near room temperature roughly doubles the rate for Eₐ ≈ 50 kJ/mol, because many more molecules cross the barrier.

Key relation

k = A e^(−Eₐ/RT) ; log(k₂/k₁) = Eₐ/(2.303R) · (1/T₁ − 1/T₂)

Set Eₐ = 50 kJ/mol and compare 298 K with 308 K.
