Electric dipole potential

Move a test point around a dipole and measure potential.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Potential is zero along the perpendicular bisector of an ideal dipole.

Key relation

V = kq(1/r+ − 1/r−)

Try this

Place the test point at 90°.

What you can change

  • Test-point angle: 0 to 180 °
  • Distance from centre: 0.4 to 1.5 m
  • Charge magnitude: 1 to 5 µC
