Molarity and dilution

Dissolve a solute, then add water and see the molarity fall while the moles of solute stay the same.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Dilution changes the volume, not the amount of solute, so concentration falls in proportion.

Key relation

M = n / V(L) ; M₁V₁ = M₂V₂

Try this

Make 100 mL of 1 M solution and dilute it to 250 mL. What is the new molarity?

What you can change

  • Moles of NaCl: 0.01 to 0.5 mol
  • Starting volume: 50 to 250 mL
  • Water added: 0 to 500 mL
