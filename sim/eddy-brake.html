Eddy-current braking

Rotate a conducting disc through a magnetic brake and follow the loss of rotational energy.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Induced currents oppose the motion that produces them. Rotational energy becomes heat without mechanical contact.

Key relation

τbrake = −bω; ω = ω₀e^(−bt/I)

Try this

Compare a weak and a strong magnetic brake.

What you can change

  • Disc moment of inertia: 0.2 to 2 kg·m²
  • Initial angular speed: 1 to 8 rad/s
  • Magnetic braking coefficient: 0.1 to 1 N·m·s
