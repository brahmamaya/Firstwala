Average vs instantaneous velocity

Shrink the time interval and watch the secant slope approach the tangent slope on the x–t graph.

Open the simulation

What to notice

For uniform acceleration the average over [t₁, t₁+Δt] equals the instantaneous velocity at the interval midpoint.

Key relation

v̄ = Δx/Δt → v = dx/dt as Δt → 0

Try this

Make Δt very small. How close do the two velocities get?
