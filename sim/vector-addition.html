Vector addition: the parallelogram

Resolve two vectors into components and build their resultant, head to tail.

What to notice

The resultant is the diagonal from the common tail. Opposite equal vectors cancel.

Key relation

R² = A² + B² + 2AB cos θ

Try this

Make the vectors equal and set the angle to 180°.

What you can change

  • Vector A magnitude: 1 to 8 units
  • Vector B magnitude: 1 to 8 units
  • Angle between vectors: 0 to 180 °
