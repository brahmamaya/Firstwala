Kirchhoff’s laws: two-loop circuit

Two cells drive currents through three resistors. Solve the loops and watch each branch current.

Open the simulation

What to notice

At the junction the currents in equal the currents out; around each loop the EMFs equal the IR drops.

Key relation

E₁ = I₁R₁ + I₃R₃ ; E₂ = I₂R₂ + I₃R₃ ; I₃ = I₁ + I₂

Try this

Make E₂ much larger than E₁. Does I₁ reverse?

What you can change

  • E₁: 1 to 12 V
  • E₂: 1 to 12 V
  • R₁: 1 to 20 Ω
  • R₂: 1 to 20 Ω
  • R₃ (shared): 1 to 20 Ω
