Spirometer: lung volumes and capacities

Breathe normally, then take a maximum breath in and out. Read the volumes and capacities from the spirogram.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Even after a forcible expiration, the residual volume of air remains in the lungs.

Key relation

VC = ERV + TV + IRV ; TLC = VC + RV ; minute volume = TV × rate

Try this

Increase the breathing rate to 16 per minute. What is the minute volume?

What you can change

  • Tidal volume TV: 300 to 800 mL
  • Inspiratory reserve IRV: 2000 to 3500 mL
  • Expiratory reserve ERV: 800 to 1300 mL
  • Residual volume RV: 900 to 1400 mL
  • Breaths per minute: 8 to 24
