Magnetic flux through a tilted coil

Tilt a coil in a uniform magnetic field and see the flux follow cos θ.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Flux is greatest when the field is perpendicular to the coil’s plane (θ = 0) and zero when it lies in the plane.

Key relation

Φ = NBA cos θ

Try this

Spin the coil and watch the induced EMF peak when the flux is zero.

What you can change

  • Magnetic field: 0.1 to 1 T
  • Coil area: 10 to 400 cm²
  • Turns: 1 to 200
  • Tilt angle θ: 0 to 180 °
  • Coil: choose Held at angle θ, Rotating at 1 rev/s
