Hinsberg test for amines

Treat a primary, secondary or tertiary amine with benzenesulphonyl chloride and then with KOH.

Open the simulation

What to notice

1°: the sulphonamide dissolves in alkali (acidic N–H); 2°: it is insoluble in alkali; 3°: no reaction.

Key relation

RNH₂ / R₂NH + C₆H₅SO₂Cl → sulphonamide + HCl
