The cardiac cycle

Watch the heart’s chambers contract and relax in sequence and hear when the valves close (lub, dub).

Open the simulation

What to notice

At 72 beats per minute one cycle lasts about 0.8 s (0.83 s): atrial systole 0.1 s, ventricular systole 0.3 s, joint diastole 0.4 s.

Key relation

Cardiac output = stroke volume × heart rate ≈ 70 mL × 72 = ~5 L/min

Try this

Raise the heart rate. Which phase gets shorter?

What you can change

  • Heart rate: 50 to 180 beats/min
  • Stroke volume: 50 to 120 mL
