The human skeleton: 206 bones

What to notice

Each limb has 30 bones; the vertebral column has 26 serially arranged vertebrae.

Key relation

206 bones = 80 (axial) + 126 (appendicular)

Try this

Add up the skull (22), hyoid (1), ear ossicles (6), vertebral column (26), sternum (1) and ribs (24). Do you get 80?
