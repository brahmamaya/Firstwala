Oxygen–haemoglobin dissociation curve

See how the % saturation of haemoglobin depends on pO₂, and how CO₂, acidity and temperature shift the curve.

Open the simulation

What to notice

In tissues (low pO₂, high pCO₂, high H⁺, higher temperature) oxyhaemoglobin dissociates and releases O₂.

Key relation

Sigmoid curve ; high pCO₂, H⁺ or temperature → shift right (O₂ released)

Try this

Raise pCO₂ and temperature together. What happens to saturation at 40 mm Hg?

What you can change

  • pO₂: 0 to 120 mm Hg
  • pCO₂: 20 to 70 mm Hg
  • Temperature: 34 to 42 °C
  • pH: 7 to 7.7
