Chordates vs non-chordates

Look inside a transparent body and compare the five key differences between chordates and non-chordates (NCERT Table 4.1).

Open the simulation

What to notice

In chordates the nerve cord is dorsal and the heart ventral — the reverse of non-chordates.

Key relation

Chordate hallmark: notochord + dorsal hollow nerve cord + pharyngeal gill slits
