Bacterial shapes and binary fission

Choose a bacterial shape and watch a colony multiply by binary fission. Each generation doubles the number of cells.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Under ideal conditions growth is exponential — numbers explode after a few hours.

Key relation

N = N₀ × 2ⁿ, n = t / g

Try this

Set g = 20 min (E. coli in rich medium). How many cells after 4 hours from one cell?

What you can change

  • Shape: choose Coccus (spherical), Bacillus (rod), Vibrio (comma), Spirillum (spiral)
  • Generation time g: 10 to 120 min
  • Time elapsed: 0 to 8 h
  • Starting cells N₀: 1 to 100
