Reaction and braking distance

Follow a car through reaction time, braking, and a complete stop.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Braking distance grows with the square of speed; reaction distance grows linearly.

Key relation

sstop = utreaction + u²/(2a)

Try this

Double the starting speed and compare the stopping distance.

What you can change

  • Initial speed: 5 to 30 m/s
  • Braking deceleration: 2 to 10 m/s²
  • Reaction time: 0.2 to 1.5 s
