Prism: angle of minimum deviation

Change the angle of incidence on a glass prism and measure the angle of emergence and the deviation.

Open the simulation

What to notice

As i increases, δ first decreases and then increases. At the minimum, i = e and the ray inside is parallel to the base.

Key relation

δ = i + e − A, n = sin[(A + δₘ)/2] / sin(A/2)

Try this

Find the angle of incidence that gives the smallest deviation. Is i = e there?

What you can change

  • Angle of incidence i: 25 to 80 °
  • Angle of prism A: 45 to 65 °
  • Refractive index of glass: 1.4 to 1.7
