Brewster’s angle

Reflect unpolarised light from glass. At Brewster’s angle the reflected beam is completely polarised.

Open the simulation

What to notice

At θ_B the reflected and refracted rays are perpendicular, and the p-component is not reflected at all.

Key relation

tan θ_B = n₂ / n₁ ; θ_B + θ_r = 90°

Try this

Find the angle where R_p falls to zero for water (n = 1.33).

What you can change

  • Refractive index n₂: 1.3 to 2.4
  • Angle of incidence: 0 to 89 °
