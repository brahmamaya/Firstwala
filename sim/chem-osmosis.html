Osmosis and osmotic pressure

Separate a solution from pure water by a semipermeable membrane and find the pressure that stops osmosis.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Water flows into the more concentrated side; applying more than π reverses the flow (reverse osmosis, used to purify water).

Key relation

π = i C R T ; R = 0.0821 L atm mol⁻¹ K⁻¹

Try this

Find π for 0.1 M glucose at 300 K. Then apply a larger pressure.

What you can change

  • Concentration: 0.01 to 0.5 mol/L
  • van ’t Hoff factor i: 1 to 3
  • Temperature: 273 to 350 K
  • Applied pressure: 0 to 30 atm
