EMF of a rotating rod

Spin a metal rod about one end in a uniform magnetic field. The free electrons are pushed outward.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Every part of the rod moves at a different speed; the average speed is ωL/2.

Key relation

ε = ½ B ω L²

Try this

Double the length. Does the EMF double or quadruple?

What you can change

  • Magnetic field: 0.1 to 2 T
  • Rod length: 0.2 to 2 m
  • Rotation rate: 10 to 3000 rpm
