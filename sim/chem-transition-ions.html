What to notice

Ions with partly filled d orbitals are coloured and paramagnetic; d⁰ and d¹⁰ ions (Sc³⁺, Zn²⁺) are colourless and diamagnetic.

Key relation

μ = √(n(n + 2)) BM (n = number of unpaired electrons)
