What to notice

Cr (3d⁵4s¹) and Cu (3d¹⁰4s¹) break the simple order because half-filled and completely filled d subshells are extra stable.

Key relation

Order: 1s < 2s < 2p < 3s < 3p < 4s < 3d < 4p (n + l rule)
