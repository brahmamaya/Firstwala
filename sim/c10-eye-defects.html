Defects of vision and their correction

See where a short-sighted or long-sighted eye focuses light, and the lens that corrects it.

Open the simulation

What to notice

A myopic eye focuses distant objects in front of the retina; a concave lens fixes it. A hypermetropic eye focuses near objects behind the retina; a convex lens fixes it.

Key relation

Myopia: P = −1 / far point (m); Hypermetropia: P = 1/0.25 − 1/near point (m)

What you can change

  • Eye: choose Normal eye, Myopia (short-sight), Hypermetropia (long-sight)
  • Far point (myopia): 0.5 to 5 m
  • Near point (hypermetropia): 0.3 to 2 m
  • Spectacles: choose Not worn, Corrective lens worn
