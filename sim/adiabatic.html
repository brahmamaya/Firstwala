Adiabatic compression and expansion

Move an insulated piston and trade mechanical work with the gas’s internal energy.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Compression raises temperature without heat entering the gas. Expansion cools it while the gas does work.

Key relation

PVᵞ = constant; TVᵞ⁻¹ = constant

Try this

Switch between half and twice the initial volume.

What you can change

  • Final / initial volume: 0.5 to 2 ×
  • Initial temperature: 250 to 500 K
  • Heat-capacity ratio: choose Diatomic gas, γ = 1.4, Monatomic gas, γ = 5/3
