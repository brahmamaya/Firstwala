Three particles chasing each other (HC Verma)

Three particles at the corners of an equilateral triangle each move at speed v straight toward the next. Find when they meet. Source: Concept of Physics, H. C. Verma.

Open the simulation

What to notice

The triangle shrinks and turns but stays equilateral; each path is a spiral that ends at the centre O.

Key relation

T = 2a / 3v (speed toward the centre = v cos 30°)

Try this

Double the side a. Does the meeting time double? Double the speed v. What happens to T?

What you can change

  • Side a: 1 to 10 m
  • Speed v: 0.5 to 5 m/s
