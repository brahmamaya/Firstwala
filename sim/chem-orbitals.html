Shapes of atomic orbitals

Pick an orbital and the principal quantum number to see its 3D shape, lobes and nodes.

Open the simulation

What to notice

p orbitals have one nodal plane through the nucleus and d orbitals have two; the two colours show opposite signs of the wave function.

Key relation

radial nodes = n − l − 1 ; angular nodes = l ; total nodes = n − 1

Try this

Compare 2p and 3p: do they have the same shape? How many radial nodes does 3p have?
