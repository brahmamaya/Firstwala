Limiting reagent: making water

Mix hydrogen and oxygen gas and find which runs out first in 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O.

Open the simulation

What to notice

The reactant that is used up first limits the amount of product; the other is left over in excess.

Key relation

2 H₂ + O₂ → 2 H₂O ; n(H₂O) = min(n(H₂), 2 n(O₂))

Try this

Use 4 g H₂ and 16 g O₂. Which one is limiting?

What you can change

  • Hydrogen taken: 0.5 to 10 g
  • Oxygen taken: 4 to 80 g
  • Reaction progress: 0 to 100 %
