Brownian motion

A pollen-sized particle is jostled by invisible molecules. Its mean-square displacement grows linearly with time.

What to notice

The path is random, but hotter, thinner liquids and smaller particles spread out faster.

Key relation

D = kT / 6πηa ; ⟨r²⟩ = 6Dt
