Helical motion in a magnetic field

Launch a proton at an angle to a magnetic field and separate circular and forward motion.

Open the simulation

What to notice

A parallel velocity adds forward travel without altering the circular component.

Key relation

r = mv⊥/(qB); pitch = v∥·2πm/(qB)

Try this

Set the angle to 90° to get a circle with zero pitch.

What you can change

  • Proton speed: 1 to 10 ×10⁵ m/s
  • Magnetic field: 0.1 to 1 T
  • Angle to field: 0 to 90 °
