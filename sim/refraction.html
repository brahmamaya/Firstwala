Refraction and total internal reflection

Send a ray across a boundary between two optical media.

Open the simulation

What to notice

When light goes toward a lower refractive index, sufficiently large angles produce total internal reflection.

Key relation

n₁sinθ₁ = n₂sinθ₂

Try this

Use n₁ = 1.5, n₂ = 1.0 and increase the angle.

What you can change

  • Incident medium index: 1 to 2
  • Second medium index: 1 to 2
  • Incidence angle: 0 to 80 °
