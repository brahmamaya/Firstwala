Atwood machine

Connect two hanging masses over a pulley and predict which side descends.

Open the simulation

What to notice

The tension is the same on both sides of an ideal pulley. Equal masses balance.

Key relation

a = (m₂ − m₁)g/(m₁ + m₂)

Try this

Make the masses equal, then increase only the right-hand mass.

What you can change

  • Left mass m₁: 1 to 8 kg
  • Right mass m₂: 1 to 8 kg
