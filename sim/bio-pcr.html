Polymerase chain reaction (PCR)

Cycle through denaturation, primer annealing and extension. Each cycle (ideally) doubles the DNA.

Open the simulation

What to notice

A thermostable DNA polymerase from Thermus aquaticus (Taq) survives the high denaturation temperature.

Key relation

Copies after n cycles = N₀(1 + E)ⁿ ; ideal (E = 1): N₀ × 2ⁿ
