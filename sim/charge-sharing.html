Sharing charge between capacitors

Connect two charged capacitors. Charge redistributes to a common voltage — and some energy is lost.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Charge is conserved but energy is not: the loss appears as heat and radiation in the connecting wires.

Key relation

V = (C₁V₁ + C₂V₂)/(C₁ + C₂) ; ΔU = C₁C₂(V₁ − V₂)² / 2(C₁ + C₂)

Try this

Make V₁ = V₂. Is there any loss now?

What you can change

  • C₁: 1 to 100 μF
  • V₁: 0 to 100 V
  • C₂: 1 to 100 μF
  • V₂: 0 to 100 V
