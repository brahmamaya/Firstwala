Glancing collision of two balls

Strike a stationary ball off-centre. Vary the impact offset and the coefficient of restitution.

Open the simulation

What to notice

For equal masses in an elastic glancing collision, the two balls leave at right angles.

Key relation

Along centres: v₂ = u cosα (1+e)/2 ; e = 1 → paths at 90°

Try this

Set e = 1 and try several offsets — measure the angle between the paths.

What you can change

  • Cue-ball speed: 1 to 6 m/s
  • Impact offset b/2r: 0 to 0.95
  • Restitution e: 0 to 1
