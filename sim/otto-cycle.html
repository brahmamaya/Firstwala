Petrol engine (Otto cycle)

Compress the fuel–air mixture more and the ideal efficiency rises — watch the piston and crank turn.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Efficiency depends only on compression ratio r and γ — not on how much fuel is burned.

Key relation

η = 1 − r^(1−γ)

Try this

Raise r from 8 to 12. How much does efficiency improve?

What you can change

  • Compression ratio r: 2 to 14
  • Heat capacity ratio γ: 1.3 to 1.67
  • Engine speed: 600 to 6000 rpm
