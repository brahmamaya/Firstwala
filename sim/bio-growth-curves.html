Arithmetic, geometric and sigmoid growth

Grow a root by arithmetic growth or a cell population by geometric growth, and see the S-shaped curve when resources limit growth.

Open the simulation

What to notice

Geometric growth starts slowly, accelerates (log phase) and then slows as nutrients run short — giving a sigmoid curve.

Key relation

Arithmetic: L_t = L₀ + rt ; geometric: W₁ = W₀e^(rt)

Compare the two growth types at the same rate r.

What you can change

  • Growth: choose Arithmetic (root elongation), Geometric → sigmoid (cell number)
