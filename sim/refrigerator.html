Refrigerator and heat pump

Pump heat from a cold box to a warm room. The ideal coefficient of performance depends only on the temperatures.

Open the simulation

What to notice

The closer the two temperatures, the less work is needed to move each joule of heat.

Key relation

COP = Q_c / W = T_c / (T_h − T_c)

Set the freezer colder. How does the required power change?

What you can change
