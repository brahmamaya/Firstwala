  • Hybridisation of carbon — Switch between sp, sp² and sp³ carbon and see the hybrid orbitals, the bond angles and the molecule they make.

Acids, Bases and Salts (4 simulations)

  • Buffer solutions (Henderson equation) — Mix acetic acid with sodium acetate and add a little strong acid or base; see how little the pH changes.
  • pH of acids and bases — Choose an acid or base and its concentration; see the pH, the indicator colour and the ions in solution.
  • Acid–base indicators — Test common solutions with litmus, phenolphthalein, methyl orange, turmeric and universal indicator.
  • Neutralisation: acid + base → salt + water — Add sodium hydroxide from a burette to 25 mL of hydrochloric acid with phenolphthalein, and follow the pH.

  • Reactivity series: displacement reactions — Dip a metal strip into a salt solution. A more reactive metal displaces a less reactive metal from its salt.
