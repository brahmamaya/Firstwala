Light – Reflection and Refraction (8 simulations)

  • Real and apparent depth — Look straight down into a pool. Refraction makes the coin on the bottom appear raised.
  • Image in a concave mirror — Move an object in front of a concave mirror and locate its image.
  • Two thin lenses in contact — Combine converging and diverging lenses and discover their equivalent optical power.
  • Refraction through a glass slab — A ray passes through a rectangular glass slab. It bends towards the normal on entering and away on leaving.
  • Image through a convex lens — Move an object toward or away from a lens and trace the rays to its image.
  • Images in two inclined mirrors — Place an object between two plane mirrors and change the angle. Count the images.
  • Refraction and total internal reflection — Send a ray across a boundary between two optical media.

Electricity (6 simulations)

  • Cells in series and parallel — Combine n identical cells in series or in parallel and compare the current through a load.
  • Ohm’s law circuit — Change battery voltage and resistance to control the current in a closed circuit.
  • Electric power and the electricity bill — Choose an appliance rating and how long it runs. Find the current, the heat produced and the monthly bill.
  • Electron drift in a wire — Change wire thickness and current to see why electron drift is so slow.
  • Series and parallel resistors — Compare equivalent resistance for two connection types.
  • Resistance of a wire — Change the material, length and thickness of a wire and see how its resistance and the current change.

The Human Eye and the Colourful World (4 simulations)

  • Defects of vision and their correction — See where a short-sighted or long-sighted eye focuses light, and the lens that corrects it.
  • Dispersion through a prism — Send white light through glass and separate its component colours.
  • Scattering of light: blue sky, red sunset — Sunlight is scattered by the air. Change the height of the Sun and the size of the particles in the air.
  • Total internal reflection — Shine a laser from glass towards air. Beyond the critical angle no light escapes.
