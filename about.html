About Physica

Learn science by playing with it.

Physica is an interactive science lab in your browser. Instead of only reading formulas, students move sliders, change values and watch the laws of physics come alive in 3D - projectiles fly, waves travel, circuits glow and planets orbit.

What you get

  • Hundreds of simulations for Class 10, 11 and 12 Physics (NCERT chapters), plus Chemistry, Botany and Zoology.
  • Physica AI Tutor - a friendly teacher-friend in English, Hinglish and हिंदी that explains at basic, average or JEE/NEET level, asks questions, gives hints, checks your answers and speaks out loud.
  • Teacher mode for classrooms and digital boards, and Student mode for self-study.
  • Works on phones, laptops, tablets and smart boards, and can be installed like an app.

Our belief

Every student - in a big city or a small town - deserves to see physics, not just memorise it. Physica is built to make concepts clear, build confidence for Boards, JEE and NEET, and make learning genuinely enjoyable.

How the AI Tutor works

The tutor runs entirely inside your browser using lessons written in advance by the Physica team. It does not send your conversation to any AI company. Questions it cannot answer yet are sent anonymously to us so that we can add good answers.
