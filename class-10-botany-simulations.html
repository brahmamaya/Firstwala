13 free interactive Class 10 Botany simulations covering 3 NCERT chapters, including How do Organisms Reproduce?, Our Environment, Heredity. Change the variables, watch the result, and learn for school exams, JEE Main and NEET.

  • Menstrual cycle: ovary, uterus and hormones — Move through a 28-day cycle and watch follicle growth, ovulation, the corpus luteum and the uterine lining.
  • Mitosis, stage by stage — Watch the chromosomes of an animal cell condense, line up, separate and form two identical nuclei.
  • Kinds and agents of pollination — Move pollen within a flower, between flowers of one plant, or between plants — by wind or by insects.
