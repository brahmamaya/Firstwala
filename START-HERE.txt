PHYSICA - NETLIFY-READY STATIC WEBSITE

DEPLOY
1. Extract this ZIP. index.html must be directly inside the extracted folder.
2. Open Netlify Drop (https://app.netlify.com/drop) and sign in if requested.
3. Drag the extracted folder containing index.html into Netlify.
No build command, backend, database, API keys, or environment variables are needed.
The ZIP itself can also be used by deployment tools that accept ZIP archives.

INCLUDED
PHYSICS: 276 adjustable Class 11 and Class 12 physics simulations across all 28
chapters. Every one is now drawn in rotatable 3D; the original flat drawings remain
one tap away with the "2D diagram" button (remembered in this browser).
CHEMISTRY: 50 chapter-wise NCERT simulations (Class 11 chapters 1–9, Class 12 chapters
1–10): mole concept, limiting reagent, orbitals, electronic configuration, periodic
trends, VSEPR, molecular orbitals, Gibbs energy, Le Chatelier, pH, redox, chromatography,
ethane conformations, Raoult’s law, colligative properties, Daniell cell, electrolysis,
reaction order, Arrhenius, 3d ions, crystal field, SN1/SN2, alcohols, acid and base
strength, glucose mutarotation, dilution, empirical formula, hydrogen spectrum, isoelectronic ions,
hybridisation, dipole moments, Hess’s law, buffers, Ksp, oxidation numbers, isomers, Markovnikov,
benzene, Henry’s law, osmosis, conductivity, catalysts, lanthanoid contraction, geometrical isomers,
chirality, phenol acidity, carbonyl addition, Hinsberg test, zwitterions (chem1.js–chem4.js).
BOTANY and ZOOLOGY: 100 adjustable Class 11 and Class 12 biology simulations arranged
chapter-wise by the NCERT syllabus, split the way NEET and board courses split them.
Botany: living world, classification, plant kingdom, morphology and anatomy of flowering
plants, cell, cell cycle, photosynthesis, respiration, plant growth, sexual reproduction
in flowering plants, inheritance, molecular basis of inheritance, microbes, ecology.
Zoology: animal kingdom, animal tissues and cockroach, biomolecules, human physiology
(breathing, heart, kidney, muscles and joints, nerves, hormones), human reproduction,
reproductive health, evolution, health and disease, biotechnology.
Every model is 3D with its parts named on the stage (organelles, heart chambers, neurons,
flower parts, chromosomes and more). Switch subject with the Physics / Botany / Zoology tabs.
LABELS AND VALUES: force, velocity, field and other vector arrows show their name, value
and a direction arrow; the current setting of every control is written on the stage.
INSTRUMENTS: the vernier caliper and the micrometer screw gauge are modelled on the real
tools (engraved scales, jaws, thimble, ratchet) with a magnified reading window.
Realistic 3D look: lit, shaded spheres and blocks with soft shadows on every
experiment (toggle with "3D look" or key 3).
3D experiments: drag the stage to orbit the camera, pinch / Ctrl+scroll / − + to zoom,
auto-spin, reset view (double-click), or switch dragging back to adjusting values.
Fine − / + steppers on every slider (hold to repeat), Randomise, Share setup links
that reopen an experiment with exact values, Snapshot (PNG), Full screen stage,
0.1×–4× playback, previous / next navigation and keyboard shortcuts (press ?).
Favourites pinned above the chapter library, stored in the current browser.
Precise numeric inputs, sliders, stage dragging, playback speed and stepping.
MAKE YOUR OWN: a simple physics sandbox (simple mode shows only the essentials and
slider controls; "Advanced tools" reveals everything). One-tap quick-start scenes (Cannon,
Basket challenge with a goal, Newton's cradle, Ramp race, Bridge, Dominoes,
Pendulums, Orbit, Ball pit); a Launch tool - pull back and let go, with a predicted
flight path; gravity chips (Earth, Moon, Mars, Jupiter, Space); material chips
(rubber, wood, metal, ice); a live graph of speed, height and kinetic / potential
energy for the selected object; shaded 3D-look objects. "Advanced tools" reveals
springs, rods, attractors, pan, world settings and the object list; rotatable rigid
bodies, collisions, toy charges, editable mass/size/velocity, undo/redo, saved
worlds, pan/zoom and touch support remain available.
Original Midnight theme plus Daylight, Violet, Chalkboard, Ink and Paper themes.

SHARING AND STORAGE
Save stores up to 30 worlds in your current browser, not in a cloud account.
Share / Export creates a snapshot link or a .physica.json file.
Friends open a link or use Import file. Imported worlds open paused for editing.
Create share links on your published Netlify domain, not the preview domain.
Large worlds should be sent as files. JSON world files are limited to 250 KB.
Browser data is separate for each domain. Export any preview saves before moving
sites. An exported file is the safest backup. Clearing browser storage removes
local favourites and saves.

SANDBOX MODEL
100 world pixels = 1 metre; fixed 10 m by 6.5 m world, up to 150 objects and
200 connections. Springs use solver stiffness, rods keep a fixed distance.
Charge/attractor forces are illustrative, not calibrated electromagnetism.
Speed is limited to approximately 24 m/s for numerical stability; high-speed
collisions are approximate. Objects without boundaries stop beyond 50 m.
All scene changes can be undone (last 50 edits); continuous playback is not
recorded by undo. Reset run restores the start of the latest Play session.
Use Pause for precise placement, then select an object to edit its properties.

TECHNICAL
Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. physica3d.js is a small built-in 3D renderer
(perspective, depth sorting, lighting, orbit camera) with no external libraries;
labkit.js and seventh.js to tenth.js hold the 100 newer 3D physics experiments;
phys3d-a.js to phys3d-d.js give the 176 original physics experiments their 3D scenes
(phys3d-inst.js: realistic vernier caliper and screw gauge);
biokit.js and bio1.js to bio5.js hold the 100 biology experiments.
Matter.js 0.20.0 and its MIT licence are included
locally in vendor/. No external scripts or CDN dependency. No React build is
needed for this archive. Use a current Chrome, Safari, Firefox or Edge browser.
