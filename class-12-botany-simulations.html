Organisms and Populations (4 simulations)

  • Age pyramids — Compare the age structure of expanding, stable and declining human populations.
  • Exponential and logistic growth — Grow a population with unlimited resources (exponential, J-shaped) or limited resources (logistic, S-shaped).
  • Population interactions — Explore the six kinds of interaction between two species and what each species gains or loses.
  • Predator–prey cycles — Prey multiply; predators eat them and multiply in turn; prey fall, predators starve — and the cycle repeats.
